Frequently asked questions

How much is a pizza?

In 30 cm between 11.0 euros and 13.5 euros, in 45 cm between 19.0 euros and 24.0 euros. The pizza bread costs 5.5 euros in 30 cm and 9.0 euros in 45 cm.

Which pizza sizes are there?

Two: 30 cm and 45 cm in diameter. Every pizza on the menu comes in both sizes.

How big is a 45 cm pizza?

It has about 2.25 times the area of the 30 cm pizza.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

The menu includes, for example, Pizza Margherita and Vegetariana, Tagliatelle con Crema di Zucchine and several salads without meat or fish. The ingredients are listed with every dish.

Can I order extra toppings?

Yes. There are 28 extra toppings for pizza. Each costs 1.0 to 2.0 euros, depending on topping and size.

Which dressings do you have?

There are four dressings: balsamic, honey mustard, orange and orange curry mayo. All of them contain garlic.

Do you sell wine?

Yes, selected Italian wines by the bottle to take away from our shop, not via the delivery services. Ask us about the current selection.
