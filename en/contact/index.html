Frequently asked questions

Where is H4 Italiafood?

At Kilianstraße 55, 90425 Nürnberg, in the Thon district.

Is H4 still open?

Yes. We are open Monday to Friday from 11:00 to 14:00 and from 17:00 to 20:00, and on Saturday from 16:00 to 20:00.

Which number can I call to order?

On 0911 377 778 17 or 0911 377 778 18. By phone we take orders for pick-up. For delivery, order via Lieferando, Uber Eats or Wolt.

Are you open on Saturday lunchtime?

No. On Saturdays we open at 16:00.

Can I order by email?

Please order by phone. That way your order is sure to reach us.

How can I find you on Instagram?

At @h4italiafood.
