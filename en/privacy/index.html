Privacy policy

This is a translation. The German version is authoritative. Read the German version

In brief

This website sets no cookies, uses no tracking and loads no content from third parties. Fonts and map are part of the website itself. Data is only processed when your browser loads the page or when you call or write to us.

1. Controller

H4 Italiafood UG (haftungsbeschränkt)
Kilianstraße 55, 90425 Nürnberg
Phone: 0911 377 778 17
Email: info@h4-italiafood.de

2. Hosting and server logs

This website is delivered via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes your IP address as well as the date and time, the page accessed, browser and operating system and the amount of data transferred, in order to deliver the page and secure the service. This data is also processed in the USA. According to GitHub, the transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission of 10 July 2023, Art. 45 GDPR) and additionally on standard contractual clauses. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable provision of the website. We ourselves have no access to these logs; GitHub decides how long they are kept. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement, https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Domain and email are hosted by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Straße 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany.

3. Cookies and local storage

We do not set cookies. If you choose a pizza size in the menu, your browser stores this choice locally on your device (local storage, entry “h4-groesse”) so that it is still there on your next visit. For the duration of your visit, your browser also notes in session storage that the opening animation and the sliding-in of the main image have already played (entries “h4-intro” and “h4-naht”), whether you have just switched language (“h4-flagge”) and whether the page transition on the first move away from the home page has already played (“h4-ue-erst”, briefly “h4-ue-start”). They are technically necessary so that the site does not replay the same thing on every page view. These entries contain no personal data, serve only the display you have chosen and are not read out or transferred (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Session storage is cleared when you close the window. You can delete all entries in your browser at any time.

4. Contact by phone and email

If you call or write to us, we process your details to handle your order or enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for general enquiries. We delete the data as soon as it is no longer needed and no statutory retention obligations apply.

5. Links to ordering platforms

We name delivery services through which you can order. As soon as we link to them, the following applies: you only leave our website when you click such a link. The privacy policy of the respective provider then applies.

6. Map and directions

The location map is an image delivered as part of this website (map data: © OpenStreetMap contributors). No data is sent to third parties. If you tap “Get directions”, Google Maps (Google Ireland Ltd.) opens. From then on, Google’s terms apply.

7. Instagram

We link to our Instagram profile (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.). It is a simple link without embedded content. Data is only transferred when you click the link.

8. Fonts

The fonts are delivered as part of this website. There is no connection to Google Fonts or other font services.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15, 16, 17, 18, 20 and 21 GDPR). To exercise these rights, write to us at info@h4-italiafood.de.

Right to object: Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). An informal message to info@h4-italiafood.de is sufficient.

You are not obliged to provide us with any data. Without a name and phone number, however, we cannot accept an order. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

We only show photos of staff with their consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). It can be withdrawn at any time.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach.
